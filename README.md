# Dr. Muhammad Mohsin

**Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Genoa, Italy**

I am a computer scientist specializing in AI-driven sustainable circular economy solutions, with a focus on electronic waste recycling and critical raw materials recovery. My research combines deep learning, computer vision, and edge computing to build intelligent systems for the automated disassembly and sorting of waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE). I also work on medical imaging analysis applications.
