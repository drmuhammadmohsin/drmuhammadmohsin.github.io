About Me

Muhammad Mohsin is a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Genova, Italy. He received his M.S. degree in Computer Engineering from NUST, Islamabad, Pakistan in 2021. He is actively involved in cutting-edge research and development in the fields of Medical Imaging, Circular Economy, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Artificial Intelligence. His current research interests include Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, AI, Medical Imaging, Urban Mines, E-Waste Recycling, and Circular Economy. Thank you for visiting my profile. Please feel free to reach out if you have any questions or ideas to share. I look forward to connecting with others who share my passion for computer science and technology.
